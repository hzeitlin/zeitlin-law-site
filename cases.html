Representative Eminent Domain Cases & Verdicts

A comprehensive record of landmark jury verdicts, published appellate precedents, and strategic condemnation settlements achieved by Dale Zeitlin against state, municipal, and federal condemning authorities.

City of Phoenix v. Crescent 2400
$5,700,000 Total Recovery
2026 Jury Trial • Office Building Condemnation
The City condemned a portion of land from an office building. The City's appraisal was $680,000. The jury awarded nearly $3.8MM, alongside $1.1MM in statutory interest and $765,000 in sanctions against the City for a total recovery of $5,700,000.
State of Arizona v. Foothills (ADOT)
$30,500,000+ Total Recovery

City of Scottsdale v. Toll Brothers
~$91,000,000 Total Award
2008 Jury Trial (3 Weeks) • 384 Acres
The City condemned approximately 384 acres for a desert preserve, appraising the property at $33,800,000. The jury awarded nearly $82,000,000 plus over $9,000,000 in statutory interest. This stands as the largest condemnation verdict in Arizona history.
City of Phoenix v. Garretson
~$4,000,000 Final Judgment
2016 Arizona Supreme Court Precedent & Jury Trial
Light rail construction eliminated access to Jefferson Street. The City appraised the take at $7,000; the jury awarded nearly $3,000,000. Mr. Zeitlin successfully argued before the Arizona Supreme Court, establishing landmark precedent that property owners hold a constitutionally protected property right of access to a particular street. Briefs and oral arguments handled directly by Mr. Zeitlin.

Maricopa County Stadium District v. Downtown Phoenix Partners

2000 Jury Trial (3 Weeks) • Baseball Stadium
Taking of a 2-acre vacant parcel at 6th and Jefferson for Bank One Ballpark. County offered $900,000. After a 3-week jury trial, the jury awarded $4,663,100 (100% of Dale Zeitlin's appraisal). Actual payment reached $7,200,000.
