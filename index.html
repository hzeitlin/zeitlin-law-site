Firm Overview

Zeitlin & Zeitlin, P.C. is an eminent domain and real estate litigation practice established in Phoenix, Arizona in 1993. The firm provides premier legal representation for property owners, businesses, and investors in complex valuation disputes, condemnation, inverse condemnation, and commercial real estate litigation.

Combining rigorous trial preparation with deep substantive knowledge of property valuation, appraisal methodology, and land use regulations, Zeitlin & Zeitlin delivers uncompromising advocacy to secure just compensation against state and municipal condemning authorities.

$15,800,000 Eminent Domain Settlement
