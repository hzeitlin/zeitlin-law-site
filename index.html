Established in 1993, Zeitlin & Zeitlin, P.C. provides premier legal representation for property owners and businesses in complex valuation disputes, takings, and commercial real estate litigation in Arizona.

Established Excellence in Property Rights

Zeitlin & Zeitlin, P.C. is an eminent domain and real estate litigation practice established in Phoenix, Arizona. For over three decades, the firm has focused exclusively on protecting the constitutional and property rights of landowners, business owners, and investors facing condemnation, inverse condemnation, and severe regulatory takings.

Led by Dale S. Zeitlin and Dawn Zeitlin, the firm combines meticulous trial preparation with deep substantive knowledge of property valuation, appraisal methodology, and land use regulations. When government agencies or condemning authorities seek to acquire private property or impair access, Zeitlin & Zeitlin delivers uncompromising advocacy to secure just compensation.

Areas of Practice

Our practice is dedicated to specialized litigation involving real estate valuation, property rights, and complex commercial disputes.

Eminent Domain & Condemnation

Representing property owners when government entities seek to condemn or acquire private land for public projects, ensuring maximum recovery of just compensation and damages to the remainder.

Inverse Condemnation

Litigating on behalf of owners whose property has been taken, damaged, or severely restricted in utility by government action without formal condemnation proceedings.

Appraisal Malpractice

Prosecuting claims against negligent or deficient real property appraisers whose professional errors result in financial harm or compromised transaction valuations.

Deficiency Litigation

Defending borrowers and guarantors in commercial deficiency litigation and contesting inflated or improper lender valuations following foreclosure.

Zoning & Land Use Disputes

Navigating complex zoning challenges, regulatory hurdles, and municipal land use disputes affecting commercial development and property rights.

Commercial Real Estate Transactions

Structuring, negotiating, and executing sophisticated commercial real estate transactions, acquisitions, and asset protection strategies.
