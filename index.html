Overview

Zeitlin & Zeitlin, P.C. is an eminent domain and real estate litigation practice established in Phoenix, Arizona in 1993. The firm provides premier legal representation for property owners, businesses, and investors in complex valuation disputes, condemnation, inverse condemnation, and commercial real estate litigation.

Combining rigorous trial preparation with deep substantive knowledge of property valuation, appraisal methodology, and land use regulations, Zeitlin & Zeitlin delivers uncompromising advocacy to secure just compensation.

Representative Matters & Verdicts

$82,000,000 Condemnation Jury Award
Secured an $82 million jury verdict in a major eminent domain valuation dispute representing commercial property owners against condemning authorities.
$15,800,000 Eminent Domain Settlement
Achieved a $15.8 million settlement resolving complex valuation, severance damages, and highest-and-best use contentions.
$12,000,000 Inverse Condemnation Recovery
Recovered $12 million in an inverse condemnation settlement for property owners impacted by municipal infrastructure actions.
$2,900,000 Loss of Access Jury Award
Secured a nearly $3 million jury verdict for commercial business owners suffering severe economic damages due to impaired highway access.
