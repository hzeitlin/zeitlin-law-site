Firm Heritage

Decades of Focused Advocacy in Property Rights

Founded in 1993, Zeitlin & Zeitlin maintains an unwavering dedication to protecting private property and securing constitutional just compensation. Government agencies and municipal authorities frequently undervalue seized property or seek to expand takings beyond statutory authorization. Our firm provides rigorous trial advocacy designed to correct these imbalances.

Led by Harry Zeitlin, the firm combines deep substantive knowledge of Arizona real estate law with meticulous economic valuation analysis. From pre-condemnation negotiations and administrative hearings to complex jury trials and appellate review, Zeitlin & Zeitlin approaches each matter with uncompromising intellectual rigor and strategic depth.

Practice Focus

Areas of Practice

01

Eminent Domain & Condemnation

Representing property owners in valuation disputes and government takings. We challenge improper public use justifications, contest restrictive development freezes, and litigate compensation disputes to ensure full constitutional recovery.

02

Inverse Condemnation & Property Rights

Defending constitutional property rights against uncompensated regulatory or physical takings by state and municipal authorities, preserving maximum asset value for owners and investors.

03

Appraisal Malpractice & Professional Negligence

Litigating flawed valuations and appraisal failures on behalf of lenders, investors, and property owners injured by inaccurate, substandard, or negligent real estate appraisals.

04

Commercial Real Estate Transactions & Zoning

Guiding land development, 1031 exchanges, commercial finance, zoning boards, planning commission hearings, variance applications, and sophisticated acquisitions or dispositions.

05

Deficiency & Real Estate Litigation

Advising and litigating complex financial and debtor-creditor disputes, deficiency balance actions, fair market value offsets, and guarantor liability following trustee sales under Arizona statutory frameworks.
