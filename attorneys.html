Firm Leadership & Counsel

Zeitlin & Zeitlin, P.C. was established in 1993 in Phoenix, Arizona, by Dale Zeitlin and Dawn Zeitlin, building a specialized practice dedicated to property rights, eminent domain, valuation disputes, and complex real estate litigation.

Dale S. Zeitlin
Founding Principal

Dale S. Zeitlin is the founding principal of Zeitlin & Zeitlin, P.C., bringing decades of specialized courtroom experience representing property owners, businesses, and investors in high-stakes condemnation and valuation trials across Arizona and nationwide.

Mr. Zeitlin has secured some of the most significant jury verdicts and recoveries in the history of Arizona eminent domain law—including the landmark $82 million jury award in Scottsdale v. Toll Brothers and a $12 million inverse condemnation recovery in Arizona v. Benross. His practice focuses entirely on protecting landowners and commercial entities against government takings, regulatory overreach, and appraisal malpractice.

Dawn Zeitlin
Attorney at Law

Dawn Zeitlin is a founding member of Zeitlin & Zeitlin, P.C., practicing in property rights advocacy, commercial real estate litigation, and valuation disputes since the firm's establishment in 1993.

Ms. Zeitlin works extensively on behalf of property owners, developers, and commercial enterprises navigating complex government acquisitions, inverse condemnation actions, and land use disputes. Her meticulous trial preparation and deep understanding of real estate valuation ensure that clients receive uncompromised advocacy and just compensation under the law.
