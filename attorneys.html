Firm Leadership & Counsel

Dale S. Zeitlin
Founding Principal

Dale S. Zeitlin is the founding principal of Zeitlin & Zeitlin, P.C., bringing over three decades of distinguished trial court experience specializing in eminent domain, condemnation, inverse condemnation, and high-stakes commercial real estate litigation in Arizona.

Throughout his career, Mr. Zeitlin has established a premier reputation for challenging condemning authorities, municipalities, and state agencies in complex valuation trials. He has successfully litigated and resolved multi-million-dollar property rights disputes, securing landmark jury verdicts and settlements—including an $82 million condemnation award and a $12 million inverse condemnation recovery—by rigorously dismantling flawed government appraisal methodologies and protecting landowners' constitutional rights under the Fifth Amendment.

Dawn Zeitlin
Attorney at Law

Dawn Zeitlin is an accomplished attorney at Zeitlin & Zeitlin, P.C., focusing her practice on property rights advocacy, complex commercial real estate litigation, appraisal malpractice, and deficiency disputes.

Ms. Zeitlin works extensively on behalf of commercial property owners, real estate developers, and business investors navigating government takings, regulatory restrictions, and valuation disputes. Her practice combines meticulous evidentiary preparation with aggressive courtroom advocacy, ensuring that clients facing commercial property displacement or severe utility impairment receive the full measure of just compensation mandated by law.
