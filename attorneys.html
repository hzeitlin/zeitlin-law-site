Firm Leadership & Counsel

Zeitlin & Zeitlin, P.C. was established in 1993 in Phoenix, Arizona, by Dale Zeitlin and Dawn Zeitlin, building a specialized practice dedicated to property rights, eminent domain, valuation disputes, and complex real estate litigation.

Dale S. Zeitlin
Founding Principal

Dale S. Zeitlin is the founding principal of Zeitlin & Zeitlin, P.C., bringing decades of specialized courtroom experience representing property owners, businesses, and investors in high-stakes condemnation and valuation trials across Arizona and nationwide.

Mr. Zeitlin has secured some of the most significant jury verdicts and recoveries in the history of eminent domain law—including the landmark $82 million jury award in Scottsdale v. Toll Brothers and a $12 million inverse condemnation recovery in Arizona v. Benross. His practice focuses entirely on protecting landowners and commercial entities against government takings, regulatory overreach, and appraisal malpractice.

Education: Legal education and advanced professional training specializing in trial advocacy and real property valuation.
Admissions: State Bar of Arizona; United States District Court; appellate and federal trial courts.
