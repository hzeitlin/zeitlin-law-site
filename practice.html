Areas of Practice

Our practice is dedicated to specialized litigation involving real estate valuation, property rights, and complex commercial disputes across Arizona.

Eminent Domain & Condemnation

Representing property owners when government entities seek to condemn or acquire private land for public projects. We challenge appraisal valuations, highest-and-best use contentions, and damages to the remainder to ensure maximum recovery of just compensation.

Inverse Condemnation

Litigating on behalf of owners whose property has been taken, damaged, or severely restricted in utility by government action or public infrastructure projects without formal condemnation proceedings.

Appraisal Malpractice

Prosecuting claims against negligent or deficient real property appraisers whose professional valuation errors result in severe financial harm or compromised transaction positions.

Deficiency Litigation

Defending commercial borrowers and guarantors in deficiency litigation and actively contesting inflated or improper lender valuations following foreclosure.

Zoning & Land Use Disputes

Navigating complex municipal zoning challenges, regulatory hurdles, and land use disputes affecting commercial development and property utility.

Commercial Real Estate Transactions

Structuring, negotiating, and executing sophisticated commercial real estate acquisitions, dispositions, and asset protection strategies.
